**Practical learning project focused on Linux and offensive security, based on the Bandit wargame from OverTheWire.** The 33 documented challenges cover everything from Linux fundamentals and command-line usage to networking, enumeration, information analysis, automation, Git, and techniques for interacting with restricted environments.

- Strengthening **Linux, Bash, networking, and problem-solving skills** through progressive challenges.
- Practical use of tools such as `SSH`, `Netcat`, `Nmap`, `OpenSSL`, `grep`, `find`, `strings`, `cron`, and `Git`.
- **33/33 levels completed and documented**, prioritizing the analysis and reasoning behind each solution.
